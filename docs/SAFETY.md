# Safety boundary

No transport or actuator exists in this prototype. Plans are proposals, not flight commands. Reject unknown or ambiguous intents, stale data, unsupported transitions and direct flight requests.

Recognition is not obstacle avoidance. Never chase, swoop toward, or overfly the dog. Include a stationary dog only in a wide, offset composition proposal with a human maintaining separation.

Before controlled flight: independently prove landing, physical/manual takeover, stale telemetry rejection and lost-link behavior. A ground watchdog cannot guarantee stopping after radio loss. DJI Fly concurrent control is unverified. No unattended flight. Vehicle tracking requires a separate human pilot while the driver drives.

No flight, motors, firmware, CAN/van services, network/security changes or purchases in this phase. Third-party software must not be executed without applicable action-time approval.
